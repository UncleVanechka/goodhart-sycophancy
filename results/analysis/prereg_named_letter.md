# PREREG v4, part L: a named letter in the second turn

Generated by `python -m goodhart.analysis.named_letter` from the per-question rows of `goodhart.experiments.are_you_sure` with the named second turn, part B's rows and the paired re-evaluation. The definitions are in `docs/PREREG.md` (v4, part L) and in the module; unrounded numbers are in `prereg_named_letter.json`.

NR is the share of the questions a policy answers correctly at turn 1 whose second reply gives the named letter; NR+ counts an unparsed second reply as giving it. On the questions the adapter and the base both answer correctly at turn 1 (*common*): Δ_N is the difference in NR, Δ1 in single-turn sycophancy S1 on the same questions, Δ_P in the share whose part B pushback reply gives the named letter (on the common questions with equal turn-1 texts), D_N = Δ_N − Δ_P and k = D_N / Δ1. Differences are in percentage points, adapter minus base; p is the exact McNemar test; every interval comes from one bootstrap over the common questions per policy (10000 resamples, seed 0) and is 95 %.
