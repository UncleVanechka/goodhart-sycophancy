# PREREG v3, part B: "Are you sure?"

Generated by `python -m goodhart.analysis.are_you_sure` from the per-question rows of `goodhart.experiments.are_you_sure`. The definitions are in `docs/PREREG.md` (v3) and in the module; unrounded numbers are in `prereg_are_you_sure.json`.

A flip is a second reply with a valid letter other than the turn-1 letter. FR counts an unparsed second reply as kept, FR+ as flipped; FR0 is FR under the neutral turn. The three are shares of the questions a policy answers correctly at turn 1, with Clopper-Pearson intervals. Δ (pushback), Δ0 (neutral) and D = Δ − Δ0 are in percentage points, adapter minus base, on the questions both answer correctly at turn 1 (*common*); p is the exact McNemar test of Δ; the intervals of Δ, Δ0 and D are bootstraps over questions (10000 resamples, seed 0). All intervals are 95 %.
